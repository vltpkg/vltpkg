## Around the dashboard

- **Dashboard** — an onboarding checklist for new accounts, usage at a
  glance, recent releases on the npm mirror, and your team's recent
  publishes.
- **Registries** — browse the npm mirror and your private packages.
- **Tokens** — create, inspect, and revoke access tokens. See
  [Authentication & Tokens](/registry/tokens).
- **Members** — invite people and manage their roles. See
  [Members & Access Control](/registry/access).
- **Settings → General** — account avatar, display name, slug, and the
  account-wide two-factor requirement for package writes.

## Registries

Every vlt account comes by default with two registries:

- **`main`** — your account's private registry, served at
  `https://registry.vlt.io/<account>/main/`. Packages published here
  are scoped to your account slug (`@<account>/...`) and readable only
  by account members. See [Publishing Packages](/registry/publishing).
- **`npm`** — a secure mirror of the public npm registry, served at
  `https://registry.vlt.io/<account>/npm/`. It is read-only and
  populated from `registry.npmjs.org`, giving your installs and CI a
  consistent, fast source for public packages.

## Accounts

An account has a display name and a **slug** — a unique, URL-safe
identifier of lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens, chosen during
account setup.

<Aside type="caution">
  The slug appears in your registry URLs and in your package scope, so
  pick it carefully.
</Aside>

You can belong to more than one account, and create additional
accounts — for example, you can have a personal account and one per
organization. Switch between them with the account switcher in the
dashboard sidebar.

## Installing through your registries

Point your client's default registry at your npm mirror, and map your
account scope to your private registry:

<Badge text="Note" variant="tip" /> You don't have to change package
managers; [find out how](/registry#set-up-your-package-manager)

<Code
  code={`$ vlt config set registry=https://registry.vlt.io/acme/npm/
$ vlt config set scoped-registries=@acme=https://registry.vlt.io/acme/main/`}
  title="Terminal"
  lang="bash"
/>

The npm equivalent:

<Code
  code={`registry=https://registry.vlt.io/acme/npm/
@acme:registry=https://registry.vlt.io/acme/main/`}
  title=".npmrc"
  lang="ini"
/>

With that in place, public packages will resolve through your mirror
and `@acme/...` packages resolve from your private registry. Both
registries accept the same account token — see
[Authentication & Tokens](/registry/tokens).

The **Setup Instructions** button in the dashboard's **Registries**
section generates these instructions for your account, tailored to
your package manager.
